## Promotion checklist

Before promoting an entry:

1. verify official upstream;
2. verify latest meaningful release or activity;
3. identify license;
4. classify type correctly;
5. identify domain and use case;
6. document known maintenance / archive status;
7. avoid marketing claims;
8. record review date;
9. add defensive or methodological context;
10. add to machine-readable catalog.
