## Promotion checklist

1. verify canonical upstream;
2. verify meaningful maintenance or explicitly mark legacy;
3. identify license / commercial model;
4. classify the project correctly;
5. identify domain and use case;
6. record archive / deprecation state;
7. remove unsupported marketing claims;
8. record review date;
9. add defensive or methodological context;
10. add machine-readable metadata.

See [Catalog Review Policy](review-policy.md).
